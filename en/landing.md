## My p5 Pet

Make a creature that reacts to you. Its body, home and personality are yours to choose.

Start by [running your pet](1A%20-%20Meet%20your%20pet.md). Then choose any part below.

## Give it controls

- [Click your pet](2A%20-%20Click%20your%20pet.md) — Choose where a click counts.
- [Press a key](2B%20-%20Press%20a%20key.md) — Give the keyboard a job.

## Give it a body

- [Shape a body](3A%20-%20Shape%20a%20body.md) — Round, wide, tall or something else.
- [Make an angular body](3B%20-%20Make%20an%20angular%20body.md) — Try a robot, rock or boxy beast.
- [Add decorations](3C%20-%20Add%20decorations.md) — A hat, spots, whiskers or wings.

## Make it move

- [Wobble from side to side](4A%20-%20Wobble%20from%20side%20to%20side.md) — Make a slow sway or an excited wiggle.
- [Bounce up and down](4B%20-%20Bounce%20up%20and%20down.md) — A gentle bob or a big bounce.
- [Follow the pointer](4C%20-%20Follow%20the%20pointer.md) — Make a curious companion.

## Give it personality

- [Switch between moods](5A%20-%20Switch%20between%20moods.md) — Give an action a reaction.
- [Surprise me](5B%20-%20Surprise%20me.md) — Make the next reaction unpredictable.
- [Give it a voice](5C%20-%20Give%20it%20a%20voice.md) — Use words to show its personality.

## Make its home

- [Draw a place to live](6A%20-%20Draw%20a%20place%20to%20live.md) — A garden, bedroom, cave or planet.
- [Invent a strange world](6B%20-%20Invent%20a%20strange%20world.md) — Underwater, in space or somewhere new.

## Test and improve

- [Meet test and change](7A%20-%20Meet%20test%20and%20change.md) — Watch someone play and improve one thing.
