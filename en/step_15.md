## 7A - Meet test and change

A pet that reacts to you is a finished first version. What would make yours more fun?

## Test

- Let someone meet your pet. Can they work out how to interact?
- Try each control. Does the pet react as you intended?
- If it moves, try the edges. Do its face and decorations stay attached?
- Can you read every message?

## Change one thing

Ask: **What surprised you? What would you try next?** Pick one change, edit your code, then test again.
